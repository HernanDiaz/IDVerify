# Cover Letter

**Prof. Jiwen Lu**
Editor-in-Chief, *Pattern Recognition Letters*

**Re:** PRLETTERS-D-26-00496R1 — "Multi-Objective Pareto hyperparameter optimization for joint detection and localization of forged identity documents"

Dear Prof. Lu and Associate Editor,

Thank you for the positive assessment of our revised manuscript. We are grateful that Reviewer 1 recommends acceptance and that Reviewer 2 considers only a minor revision to be needed, and we appreciate the editorial checklist. We are pleased to submit this minor revision. As the Associate Editor and Reviewer 2 noted, no new experiments were required; all changes are editorial and interpretive.

Summary of the changes made in this round:

- **Sec. 4.4 (Pareto vs. scalar selection).** The discussion has been recalibrated to state exactly which metrics reach significance after Holm correction and which do not, together with their effect sizes and practical magnitude (Associate Editor; Reviewer 2, point 1).
- **Abstract and Discussion.** We now acknowledge the bounded, in-domain scope of the reported performance and the near-chance zero-shot transfer to SIDTD, and we note in the main text that standard augmentation does not close this cross-domain gap (Associate Editor; Reviewer 2, points 2 and 3).
- **Conclusion.** Expanded to reflect both the strengths and the limitations of the work, and made distinct in content from the abstract (editorial checklist, item 2).
- **Supplementary material.** The computational-cost breakdown has been re-added (Sec. S9), so all quantitative justifications now live in the supplement rather than only in the response letter (Reviewer 2, point 3).
- **References.** We reduced the visible arXiv presence (two preprints replaced by their published proceedings, and residual arXiv identifiers removed from five already-published entries) and added three recent *Pattern Recognition Letters* papers, each discussed individually, trimming the list to 32 items (editorial checklist, items 3 and 4). Reviewer 1's suggestion of a fine-tuned-TruFor footnote in Sec. 4.5 has also been incorporated.

The manuscript remains within the eight-page limit and the required single-spaced, two-column format, and the title is 13 words. A point-by-point response to every comment is provided in the accompanying response letter, and all changes made in this round are highlighted in blue in the revised PDF.

We hope the revised manuscript is now suitable for publication, and we thank you for your time and consideration.
