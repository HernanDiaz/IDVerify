\section{Discussion}
\label{sec:discussion}

\subsection{Analysis of Key Findings}

The ablation reveals an asymmetry between the two tasks. The
\texttt{seg\_only} variant achieves competitive Dice ($0.867 \pm 0.023$, $p =
0.288$ vs.\ Multitask) but its PR-AUC collapses to $0.728 \pm 0.053$:
the U-Net decoder captures sufficient forgery evidence for pixel-level
prediction yet cannot reliably aggregate it into a binary document-level
decision. The result confirms that the shared encoder, regularized by the
segmentation loss, learns spatially-aware features that strongly improve
document-level detection.

Comparing Multitask against \texttt{unweighted\_losses} isolates the
contribution of HPO loss weighting. These variants differ only in whether
$\lambda_{\text{mask}}$ is set by Pareto HPO (mean $2.39 \pm 0.43$) or fixed
at $0.5$. Pareto-optimized weights yield significantly higher Dice ($p = 1.3
\times 10^{-4}$, $d = 1.01$, large effect) while maintaining equivalent
PR-AUC, confirming that $\lambda_{\text{mask}} \approx 2.4$ is broadly optimal
and that MOTPE surfaces this value reliably across folds.

\subsection{Limitations}

\paragraph{Architecture.}
Richer pretrained backbones~\citep{dosovitskiy2021vit} are expected to further
improve cross-domain generalization but, given their higher training cost, would
make full nested-CV HPO challenging with current computational resources.

\paragraph{Localization variance.}
The high Track~2 standard deviation ($\pm 0.096$), driven by bonafide
false-positive masks, represents a practical reliability concern. Targeted
calibration strategies are a natural extension.
