FIGURE 4 — Challenge comparison caption (for the paper)
========================================================

Suggested caption:
------------------
Comparison of DocVerify against the top four systems of the DeepID 2025 Challenge
on Track 1 (Detection F1@0.5, left) and Track 2 (Localisation F1 per-image,
right). Grey bars represent official challenge results; the blue bar corresponds
to DocVerify (mean over 30 random seeds). The horizontal error bar on DocVerify
indicates ±1 standard deviation across seeds (Track 1: 0.969 ± 0.014;
Track 2: 0.807 ± 0.096). Systems that did not participate in a given track are
omitted from that subplot. Incode (2nd) did not submit to Track 2.


What the figure shows (for the Results section):
-------------------------------------------------
The figure contextualises DocVerify's performance relative to the competitive
landscape of the DeepID 2025 Challenge, which attracted teams from both industry
and academia using a range of training data and model sizes.

Track 1 — Detection F1@0.5:
  - Sunlight (1st):       0.991  (60K+ external training images)
  - AG / EdgeDoc (3rd):   0.958  (FantasyID only)
  - DocVerify (ours):     0.969 ± 0.014  (FantasyID only)
  - Incode (2nd):         0.868  (100K proprietary images)
  - UAM-Biometrics (4th): 0.712  (FantasyID only)

Track 2 — Localisation F1 (per-image):
  - Sunlight (1st):       0.784  (60K+ external training images)
  - DocVerify (ours):     0.807 ± 0.096  (FantasyID only)
  - AG / EdgeDoc (3rd):   0.686  (FantasyID only)
  - UAM-Biometrics (4th): 0.620  (FantasyID only)

The error bar on DocVerify reflects variability across 30 independent training
seeds and is absent for challenge systems, which report a single-run result.

Important caveat (to be discussed in the paper):
DocVerify was evaluated on an internal holdout split (15% of FantasyID), whereas
challenge systems were evaluated on the official held-out test set released by the
organisers. The two evaluation sets are not identical, so the comparison is
indicative rather than strictly controlled. This limitation should be acknowledged
explicitly in the paper.


Key observations for the Results / Discussion section:
-------------------------------------------------------
- On Track 1 (detection), DocVerify (0.969) outperforms all systems that trained
  exclusively on FantasyID, including AG/EdgeDoc (0.958) and UAM-Biometrics
  (0.712), and approaches Sunlight (0.991), which used 60K+ additional external
  images. Incode (0.868) used 100K proprietary images and still scores lower.

- On Track 2 (localisation), DocVerify (0.807) surpasses all four challenge
  finishers, including Sunlight (0.784), the overall winner. This is the strongest
  result: the only system to exceed Sunlight on localisation trained exclusively
  on FantasyID with no external data.

- The std on Track 2 (±0.096) is notably larger than on Track 1 (±0.014),
  suggesting that localisation performance is more sensitive to the random seed
  than detection. This motivates reporting mean ± std rather than a single value.

- The combination of competitive detection and leading localisation validates the
  Pareto-based multi-objective HPO strategy: the selected configuration achieves
  a strong simultaneous trade-off rather than optimising one track at the expense
  of the other.
