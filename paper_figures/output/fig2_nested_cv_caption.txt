FIGURE 2 — Nested CV results caption (for the paper)
=====================================================

Suggested caption:
------------------
Per-fold test metrics from the 10-fold nested cross-validation. Blue bars show
Validation PR-AUC and orange bars show Validation Dice (global mask overlap)
for each outer fold (F0–F9). Dashed horizontal lines indicate the cross-fold
mean for each metric: PR-AUC mean = 0.9921 ± 0.0060 and
Dice mean = 0.8555 ± 0.0345. The y-axis is truncated at 0.75 to emphasise
fold-to-fold variation. All 10 folds share the same HPO procedure (50 Optuna
MOTPE trials on the inner validation split); no fold result informs any other.


What the figure shows (for the Methods section):
-------------------------------------------------
The figure presents the generalisation performance of DocVerify evaluated under
a 10-fold nested cross-validation (NCV) scheme. The outer loop partitions the
FantasyID training set into 10 non-overlapping stratified splits; each split
serves once as the held-out test set while the remaining 9 folds are used for
inner-loop HPO (5 inner folds × 50 Optuna trials) and final model training.

This design ensures that the HPO process is entirely blind to the test fold:
hyperparameter selection (via the Pareto distance-to-ideal criterion, see Fig. 1)
is performed on the inner validation set, and the chosen configuration is
retrained from scratch on all 9 training folds before evaluation on the test
fold. Consequently, each bar in the figure is an unbiased estimate of
generalisation performance.

Two complementary metrics are reported per fold:
- PR-AUC (Precision-Recall Area Under the Curve): measures detection quality
  at the image level, aggregated across all classification thresholds.
- Dice coefficient (global): measures localisation quality as the harmonic mean
  of pixel-level precision and recall between the predicted and ground-truth
  forgery masks.


Key observations for the Results section:
------------------------------------------
- PR-AUC is consistently high across all folds (0.9769–0.9993), with a mean of
  0.9921 ± 0.0060, indicating that the detection head generalises reliably
  regardless of the data partition.
- Dice scores show slightly more fold-to-fold variability (0.8001–0.9091, mean
  0.8555 ± 0.0345), reflecting the inherent difficulty of pixel-level
  localisation on diverse forgery patterns.
- No fold exhibits a performance collapse, confirming that the model does not
  overfit to any particular subset of the training distribution.
- The small standard deviations on both metrics support the robustness of the
  nested CV design and the stability of the Pareto-based HPO strategy.
