FIGURE 1 — Pareto front caption (for the paper)
================================================

Suggested caption:
------------------
Validation objective space across all 500 HPO trials (10 outer folds × 50 Optuna
trials per fold). Each grey point represents one evaluated hyperparameter
configuration; blue points denote the 50 Pareto-optimal trials (non-dominated
solutions across folds). The dashed step-line traces the global Pareto frontier
computed from all trials. The red diamond at (1, 1) marks the ideal point
(simultaneous perfect PR-AUC and Dice), with dotted lines indicating its
projection onto each axis. The annotated point corresponds to the configuration
selected for final training, chosen as the Pareto-optimal trial minimising the
Euclidean distance to the ideal point (d = 0.316). A lower distance indicates a
better simultaneous trade-off between detection and localisation quality.


What the figure shows (for the Methods section):
-------------------------------------------------
The figure illustrates the multi-objective hyperparameter optimisation (HPO)
landscape of the DocVerify training procedure. For each of the 10 outer folds of
the nested cross-validation, 50 candidate configurations are evaluated using the
inner validation set, yielding 500 points in the (PR-AUC, Dice) objective space.

A configuration A dominates configuration B if A achieves equal or higher values
on both objectives and strictly higher on at least one. The Pareto-optimal set
(blue points) comprises all non-dominated configurations: no other trial
simultaneously outperforms them on both PR-AUC and Dice.

From each fold's Pareto front, the configuration closest to the ideal point (1, 1)
in Euclidean distance is selected for final model training:

    d = sqrt( (1 - PR-AUC)^2 + (1 - Dice)^2 )

This criterion assigns equal weight to both objectives, consistent with the
DeepID 2025 Challenge evaluation protocol, which ranks systems independently on
Track 1 (detection) and Track 2 (localisation).

The global frontier line (dashed, grey) is derived post-hoc from all 500 trials
for visualisation purposes only; model selection was performed per-fold.


Key observations for the Results section:
------------------------------------------
- The Pareto front spans a meaningful range of trade-offs: configurations with
  high PR-AUC (>0.96) tend to achieve lower Dice (~0.64), while balanced
  configurations achieve PR-AUC ~0.94 with Dice ~0.69.
- The selected configuration (d = 0.316) lies near the centre of the frontier,
  confirming that the distance-to-ideal criterion avoids extreme solutions.
- The density of grey points in the upper-right region indicates that MOTPE
  (Multi-objective Tree-structured Parzen Estimator) efficiently focused the
  search towards high-quality regions without exhaustive grid exploration.
